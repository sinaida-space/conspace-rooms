# CONSPACE ROOMS

CONSPACE ROOMS is an interactive web installation created by Sinaida Krivchenko ([sinaida.eu](https://sinaida.eu)), a new media artist, who designed the experience and wrote the code. It presents SOULS, a series of artworks by UVALISS, the artist Alisa Feer ([uvaliss.ru](https://uvaliss.ru/)).

Live: <https://conspace-rooms.vercel.app/> (Russian and English)

## What it is

Eighteen works from the SOULS series hang in an endless labyrinth of half-lit rooms that the browser builds as you walk. SOULS is a series about looking into your own inner world and accepting it as it is, dedicated to the artist's grandmother. The labyrinth follows the same road in three stages:

- **Fear:** Soviet hospital corridors, green oil paint under whitewash, humming tubes, abandoned beds and drip stands.
- **Memory:** a grandmother's flat, rose wallpaper, rugs on the walls, a velvet lampshade over a table, a television left on.
- **Acceptance:** pale rooms that dissolve into lace and light.

Portals move the visitor from one stage to the next. Every visit builds a new labyrinth. Souls in grandmother's room ask questions; a rose in the corner of the screen grows with every work seen, and with all eighteen a tunnel of roses ends the walk with a card of the questions the visitor was asked. The music is generated live: lo-fi, synth and muffled techno in the hospital, gramophone waltzes, tangos, estrada and romances in grandmother's world.

## How to experience it

Open the site in a recent browser with WebGL2, put on headphones and go slowly. Walk with the keyboard (W/S or arrows, Shift to run, E to look closely at a work), with on-screen buttons or by holding and dragging on a touch screen, or with hand gestures through the webcam. A gallery mode for projection installations lives at `/gallery`.

## Privacy

No analytics, no accounts, no cookies beyond two local settings. In gesture mode the webcam image is processed only in the browser and never recorded or sent anywhere.
