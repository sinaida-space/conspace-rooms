## Texts for forms

Most forms ask for the same five things. The Russian versions are on /press.

**Title:** CONSPACE ROOMS

**Year, format:** 2026. Browser work and gesture-controlled installation.

**Credits:** Concept, experience design and code: Sinaida Krivchenko.
SOULS series works © UVALISS (Alisa Feer).

**One line (for games festivals):** A labyrinth of half-lit rooms in the
browser that hides eighteen paintings from the SOULS series; find them all and
an arch of roses lets you out.

**One line (for art prizes):** A walk-through web installation that hangs the
SOULS series by UVALISS in a labyrinth built anew for each visitor, from
hospital corridors of fear to rooms of light.

**Artist statement, about 100 words:** Paintings usually wait in rooms you have
to travel to. CONSPACE ROOMS opens in any browser and lets a visitor meet
eighteen works alone, at their own pace, in an order no one else will repeat.
The labyrinth follows the arc of the SOULS series, from trauma to accepting
oneself: late Soviet hospital corridors, a grandmother's flat, pale rooms that
dissolve into lace. There is no map; candles, sound and a growing rose guide
the walk. In a gallery the same piece becomes a projection steered by hand
gestures. It keeps nothing about its visitors.

**Technical line:** Runs in a current desktop or phone browser with WebGL2; no install.
Installation needs a projector, a computer, a webcam and speakers (see the
rider); after the first run it works offline.
